How a TCSPC Raman LiDAR finds hydrogen at 11 m

Schematic of the laboratory system in Lockwood, Wang & Ironside (Opt. Laser Technol. 203, 115721): a 355 nm pulse crosses an H₂ plume, Raman photons return, and a time tagger builds range histograms.

Illustrative, not to scale. Photon dots are exaggerated: in the real system most laser pulses yield no detection in a given 50 ps bin (≈10⁻⁴–10⁻³ photons/pulse/bin). Histogram shapes are generated from peak counts reported in the paper (Figs. 3, 4, 12, 13), with Poisson noise; they are not the raw data. LOD ≈ 137 ppm (3σ) for the 1800 ppm case, 600 s integration.
